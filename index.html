About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Amsterdam and the Tinbergen Institute.

I use administrative and macroeconomic data to study housing markets and fiscal policy. My research examines the relationship between house prices and rents, the role of socio-economic factors and credit conditions in the Dutch housing market, and the effects of tax-based consolidation on local labour markets in Italy.

Research interests: Housing Markets, Immigration, and Fiscal Policy.

Feel free to contact me at f.paoloni2@uva.nl.
